When to reach out

We're the right call for serious policy work

Whether you're a government team working through a complex design problem, a civil society organisation that needs rigorous analytical support, or a foundation looking for research partnerships — we want to hear from you.

We don't do quick-turnaround opinion pieces or back-of-envelope estimates. We do research that holds up, analysis that travels, and advice that accounts for how policy actually works in practice.

Good reasons to get in touch
  • You need policy analysis on tax design, property taxation, or fiscal reform
  • You're designing or evaluating an income support or transfer program
  • You want a research partner who will stay involved through implementation
  • You need an expert witness or advisory voice with academic credibility
  • You're interested in co-designing research with affected communities
  • You have a grant or partnership opportunity that fits our research agenda
🏛

Both researchers are based at the Department of Economics, University of Calgary. All consulting and research engagements are conducted in accordance with University of Calgary policies.
